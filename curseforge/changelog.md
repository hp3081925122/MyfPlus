## Meet Your Fight Plus 0.1.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge). Requires Meet Your Fight and Curios API.

- Enhanced versions of all four Meet Your Fight bosses: Ancient Swampjaw, Resonant Bellringer, High-Roller Dame Fortuna and Rosalyne, Blade of Sunset. Each has a new two-phase moveset and its own pixel-art effects. The original bosses are unchanged.
- Four new summon items, crafted only from the original bosses' drops.
- Enhanced bosses drop the original boss loot plus one exclusive trophy.
- Four Curios accessories made from the trophies: Fossil Fang Bracelet, Resonant Belt Bell, House Die Ring and Sunset Rose Cloak.
- Own creative tab. Skill damage, cooldowns, skill parameters and accessory values are configurable in `myfplus-common.toml`; screen shake, boss fog and phase banners in `myfplus-client.toml`.

## 迎战增强 0.1.0（首次发布）

适用于 Minecraft 1.20.1（Forge）。前置：Meet Your Fight、Curios API。

- 为迎战的四个 Boss 各加入强化版：远古沼泽巨颌怪、万钟敲钟人、豪赌福尔图娜女爵、落日之锋罗莎琳。每个都有全新的两阶段技能组和专属像素特效。本体 Boss 保持原样。
- 新增 4 个召唤物，只能用本体 Boss 的掉落物合成。
- 强化 Boss 掉落本体 Boss 的全部掉落物，另外固定掉落 1 个专属物品。
- 专属物品可合成 4 件 Curios 饰品：化石獠牙手镯、共鸣腰铃、庄家骰戒、落日玫瑰披风。
- 独立的创造物品栏。技能伤害、冷却、技能参数和饰品数值可在 `myfplus-common.toml` 中配置；镜头震动、Boss 雾气和阶段横幅可在 `myfplus-client.toml` 中开关。
